Good to know

Questions people ask us.

Short answers. If yours is not here, call us or send a message below.

Who is Frapp & Capp for?

People with disability who want a real job. For many of our team it is their first one. You do not need to have worked in a cafe before.

Do I get paid?

Yes. Once you finish training you are employed at award wages, on a proper roster. It is not a volunteer or work-experience arrangement.

What will I learn?

Real barista skills: grinding, dialling in, milk texture and front of house. Before that, we sort out goals, support needs, travel and money-management skills together.

Where and when are shifts?

At the cafe at 1 West Terrace, Bankstown NSW 2200. The cafe is open Mon to Fri, 9:30am to 3pm, and closed on weekends.

Can someone help me apply?

Yes. Family, friends, support workers and support coordinators are welcome to fill in the form with you, send it for you, or call us on 1300 998 885 on your behalf.
