The craft

Trained on the tools that matter, not the ones that are easy.

Our baristas complete a full training pathway before they ever pull a shot for a customer. It is a real job with real standards, because that is what respect looks like.

  1. We start with the person

    Goals, support needs, and travel and money-management skills, sorted out before anyone touches an espresso machine.

  2. We train real barista skills

    Grinding, dialling in, milk texture, front-of-house: a proper course, not a token placement.

  3. We employ at award wages

    A genuine job, on the roster, at the cafe on West Terrace, not a simulation of one.
