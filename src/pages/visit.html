Getting here

West Terrace, right by the station.

Bankstown is well served by trains and buses, and the cafe is on West Terrace, a short walk from the interchange.

  • By train or bus

    Bankstown station and the bus interchange are a short walk away. Come out on the West Terrace side and follow the street.

  • By car

    There is street parking nearby. Allow a few minutes to find a spot on a weekday morning.

  • Step-free entry

    Barrier-free access at the door, wide pathways inside and a lowered counter section for wheelchair users, on both sides of the machine.

What to expect

Built so everyone can take their time.

The cafe is designed for the team who work here first, and it turns out that makes it a better place to drink coffee too.

  • Barrier-free access

    Wide, uncluttered pathways and a lowered counter section for wheelchair-using baristas and guests.

  • Clear, calm menu boards

    High-contrast, large-type signage with simple illustrations next to every item. This website is built the same way.

  • Take your time

    No rush culture here. Sit, work, chat, or just watch the machine do its thing.
