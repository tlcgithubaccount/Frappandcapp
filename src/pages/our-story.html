Our story

Coffee was never really the point.

Frapp & Capp is a social enterprise cafe from {{parentName}}, built to close a gap most people never see: how hard it is for someone with a disability to land a first real job. We picked coffee because everyone understands a good cup, and because the skills it teaches translate everywhere.

How it works

Three steps, one pathway.

Every barista at Frapp & Capp walks the same three steps, in the same order. It is a real job with real standards, because that is what respect looks like.

The order matters. Nobody is put in front of a customer to prove a point. By the time someone pulls their first shot for you, they have done the training and they are on the roster like anyone else.

Who we are

An initiative of {{parentName}}.

Frapp & Capp is run by {{parentName}}, a registered NDIS provider working across New South Wales. The cafe opened its doors on West Terrace on 10 May 2023 and has been a working, paying workplace ever since.

A portion of every sale, in the cafe and of every bag of beans, goes back into the TLC Impact Foundation, which supports more employment pathways for people with disability.
